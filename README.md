# AdventureWorks Sales Dashboard

## Project Description

The AdventureWorks Sales Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed using Microsoft Power BI. The main objective of this project is to analyze sales performance and generate meaningful business insights from the AdventureWorks dataset.

This project focuses on analyzing revenue, cost, profit, customer contributions, product performance, and regional sales performance through interactive dashboards and visualizations.

### Project Objectives

* Analyze overall business performance using key performance indicators (KPIs).
* Track revenue trends and monthly sales growth.
* Identify the top 10 revenue-generating products.
* Compare product revenue, cost, and profitability.
* Analyze customer contributions and identify top customers.
* Understand country-wise revenue and regional profitability.
* Evaluate business performance through interactive visualizations.

### Dashboard Modules

**1. Business Overview**

* Total Revenue
* Total Cost
* Total Profit
* Total Customers
* Total Orders
* Profit Margin %
* YTD Revenue and MTD Revenue
* Revenue Trend Over Time
* Revenue vs Previous Month

**2. Product Analysis**

* Top 10 Products by Revenue Contribution
* Revenue vs Cost Comparison
* Product Revenue Details
* Product Profitability Analysis

**3. Customer & Regional Analysis**

* Top 10 Revenue-Generating Customers
* Profit Contribution by Region
* Revenue Contribution by Country
* Revenue and Profit Margin Analysis

### Tools & Technologies

* Microsoft Power BI
* Power Query (ETL)
* DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
* AdventureWorks Dataset

### Project Outcome

This project helped develop practical skills in data transformation, data modeling, DAX calculations, KPI development, and interactive dashboard creation. It demonstrates how business data can be converted into meaningful insights to support data-driven decision-making.
